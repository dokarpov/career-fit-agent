# Contacts and outreach

## Contact sources

1. Human recruiter emails already present in Gmail.
2. LinkedIn profiles explicitly opened by the user and captured with the browser extension.
3. Company team / careers pages captured by the browser extension.
4. Manual CSV / contact entry.

The product does not depend on unauthorized bulk LinkedIn scraping.

## Contact roles to seek

Per role:
- hiring manager / product leader;
- direct recruiter / talent partner;
- one relevant product peer;
- warm connection when available.

## Outreach generation

Drafts are built from:
- the exact role;
- one unresolved question;
- 1–2 verified proof cards;
- relationship context.

They are never sent automatically.

A good message tries to reduce uncertainty, not merely announce an application.
