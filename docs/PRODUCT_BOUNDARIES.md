# Product boundaries

The source project explicitly says not to build this as:

- AI CV builder
- AI cover-letter generator
- generic portfolio builder
- generic job-search copilot

The wedge is:

**evidence orchestration + fit diagnosis + proof packaging + conversion learning**

## What the public prototype proves

- product architecture;
- structured decision logic;
- retrieval boundary;
- provenance-aware matching;
- human fallback;
- evaluation discipline;
- working prototype.

## What it does not prove

- PMF;
- paid traction;
- statistical fit-score validity;
- production-grade semantic search;
- enterprise integrations.
