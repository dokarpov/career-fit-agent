# Prompt contract — human review

You are reviewing an ambiguous evidence match.

Ask only what is required to decide one of:

- approve as verified;
- keep as adjacent / interview-only;
- reject as unsupported.

Review:
1. Is the artifact real?
2. Is authorship / ownership clear?
3. Does it directly support the role requirement?
4. Is the metric measured, modeled, or team-level?
5. What exact boundary must remain visible?

Do not reward confidence of wording. Reward provenance.
