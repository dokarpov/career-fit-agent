COMMON QUESTIONS

Clear product.
Clear boundaries.

Does it auto-apply to jobs? +

No. Discovery, analysis and preparation can be automated. Final submission and any external representation of your experience remain under human control.

Is this another AI résumé writer? +

No. RoleHint starts with the decision to apply and an evidence map. It creates a pack only after a role and its underlying evidence have been reviewed.

Can it help if I’m changing careers? +

It can identify transferable work and surface evidence gaps without treating experience in one domain as proof of experience in another.

Are these real roles or results? +

No. Website examples and activity counts are fictional. The prototype does not yet have validated placement-rate improvements or independently established product-market fit.

Can AI agents understand this website? +

Yes. The site includes semantic HTML, structured product data, a machine-readable product summary and public documentation linked to its repository.
