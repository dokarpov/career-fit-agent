Early-access privacy

The early-access form asks for your name, email and an optional description of the roles you seek. The form is submitted to FormSubmit, a third-party form processing service, which forwards submissions to the project owner by email. Do not include sensitive personal information or a CV in the optional field.

We use your submitted contact details to respond to your request and discuss future access. No payment data is collected by this form. To request deletion of your email submission, contact dokarpov@gmail.com. The website may cache public site files locally for installation and offline access; the form submission itself is not cached.
