ROLEHINT / PRIVACY

Privacy policy.

This notice explains how the RoleHint website handles early-access enquiries, how the public product preview works, and where third-party services may process information.

Effective date: 12 October 2026. Applies to: the RoleHint public website and its experimental browser-based workspace.

Short version: An early-access request provides your name, email address and optionally what sort of work you're looking for. A third-party form service processes your submission and sends it to the project operator. The public vacancy examples are fictional. You do not need to upload a CV to join the waiting list.

1. Who is responsible

RoleHint is an independent product prototype. The project operator is responsible for deciding why and how early-access contact details are used. A dedicated RoleHint contact email and the formal controller identification will be published after the project mailbox has been provisioned and verified. Until then, privacy-related requests can be submitted through the early-access contact form: begin the optional message with “Privacy request:”. You can request information without joining a marketing list.

Before full commercial launch: the controller's complete legal identification and direct contact address must be added here. This page is not a substitute for those disclosures.

2. What information is collected

  • Early access: the name, email address and optional career-search description you enter, plus technical data that the form provider may process for routing and anti-spam.
  • Public site: GitHub Pages and other infrastructure operators may process ordinary connection information such as IP address, browser characteristics and access logs. We do not operate a first-party analytics dashboard or ad-tracking pixels in this static landing page.
  • Installed website: the service worker may cache public HTML, styles, scripts, images and other public resources on your device to support repeat visits. It does not queue early-access submissions offline.
  • Experimental workspace: if you use the optional CV onboarding directly, the browser may extract document text and save it, along with your preferences, to localStorage on that device. It is not sent to a RoleHint backend by the current static demo. Browser document parsers can load software from external CDNs.

Do not enter health details, government IDs, financial account data or other sensitive information in the contact form. Avoid using experimental CV onboarding on shared devices.

3. Why we use it and the legal basis

Early-access information is used to receive your enquiry, assess whether your stated interest fits the proposed product, reply to you and, if appropriate, invite you to test RoleHint. The intended GDPR lawful basis for handling an enquiry you initiate is steps taken at your request before potentially providing a service (Article 6(1)(b)), or legitimate interests in responding to that enquiry (Article 6(1)(f)), depending on its content.

Submitting an enquiry does not authorize unrelated promotional mailing. A recurring marketing newsletter would require a separate appropriate opt-in and an unsubscribe mechanism. We do not sell submitted contact details or use the information for automated hiring decisions.

4. Services, recipients and transfers

The current form posts to FormSubmit, which provides submission handling, spam filtering and forwarding to an operator-controlled email inbox. Its own controls, storage practices, subprocessors and possible data transfers are governed by its terms and privacy information. The form may use CAPTCHA services to prevent abuse.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Email handling uses the receiving mailbox provider. PDF/DOCX extraction in the optional experimental workspace may load browser-side libraries through a CDN. Depending on these providers' infrastructure, personal information may be processed outside the EEA; the specific locations and applicable safeguards must be verified before production launch.

5. How long information is kept

Contact enquiries are kept only while necessary to respond and administer access requests, followed by deletion or anonymization when no longer needed, subject to applicable legal obligations. A fixed operator-side deletion schedule is not yet implemented or verified; it must be defined before accepting production sign-ups at scale. FormSubmit documents a submission archive retention period of up to 30 days, which is separate from copies forwarded to the receiving mailbox.

Information stored in the experimental workspace's browser localStorage remains there until cleared by the browser or removed by the user or application. Uninstalling a website shortcut may not clear browser storage automatically.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may request access to information about you, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to relevant processing. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, including Spain's Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD), where applicable.

To exercise your rights before the dedicated mailbox is activated, use the contact form and begin the optional message with “Privacy request:”. Include the email address used in your original submission. We may need reasonable verification before changing or deleting records. This route currently uses the same third-party form processor.

7. Security and limits

The public site is served over HTTPS. Applications pass through an external form processor, rather than a first-party secure account area. No online transmission is completely risk-free. The experimental workspace has no production authentication or account-level data recovery, and its demo opportunity data is fictional. Do not use it to submit sensitive documents or rely on it as an employment eligibility determination.

8. Children

RoleHint is designed for adults exploring employment. The early-access form is not intended for children, and we do not knowingly solicit children's personal information.

9. Changes to this notice

This policy may change as the prototype becomes a live service or its processors change. Material changes to collection, retention or marketing practices should be reflected on this page before those practices begin. The effective date above will be revised accordingly.
