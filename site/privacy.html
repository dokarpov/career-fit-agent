ROLEHINT / PRIVACY

Privacy policy.

This notice explains how the RoleHint website handles early-access enquiries, how the public product preview works, and where third-party services may process information.

Effective date: 12 October 2026. Applies to: the RoleHint public website and its experimental browser-based workspace.

Short version: An early-access request provides your name, email address and optionally what sort of work you're looking for. A third-party form service processes your submission and sends it to the project operator. The public vacancy examples are fictional. You do not need to upload a CV to join the waiting list.

1. Who is responsible

RoleHint is an independent product prototype. The project operator is responsible for deciding why and how early-access contact details are used. The operator must publish their legally required identification, a direct working contact email, and where applicable a business address. These particulars have not been verified and the notice is not fully compliant until they are supplied. While those details are being completed, you may send an enquiry via the early-access form with the optional message beginning “Privacy request:”. This is only an interim route, not a substitute for a direct legal contact channel. You can request information without joining a marketing list.

Before full commercial launch: the controller's complete legal identification and direct contact address must be added here. This page is not a substitute for those disclosures.

2. What information is collected

  • Early access: the name, email address and optional career-search description you enter, plus technical data that the form provider may process for routing and anti-spam.
  • Public site: GitHub Pages and other infrastructure operators may process ordinary connection information such as IP address, browser characteristics and access logs. We do not operate a first-party analytics dashboard or ad-tracking pixels in this static landing page.
  • Installed website: the service worker may cache public HTML, styles, scripts, images and other public resources on your device to support repeat visits. It does not queue early-access submissions offline.
  • Experimental workspace: if you use the optional CV onboarding directly, the browser may extract document text and save it, along with your preferences, to localStorage on that device. It is not sent to a RoleHint backend by the current static demo. Browser document parsers can load software from external CDNs.

Do not enter health details, government IDs, financial account data or other sensitive information in the contact form. Avoid using experimental CV onboarding on shared devices.

3. Why we use it and the legal basis

Early-access information is used to receive your enquiry, assess whether your stated interest fits the proposed product, reply to you and, if appropriate, invite you to test RoleHint. The appropriate GDPR lawful basis for responding to an enquiry will depend on the circumstances: Article 6(1)(b) where the request concerns steps before a prospective service relationship, or Article 6(1)(f) for legitimate interests in handling general business enquiries. The controller must document its assessment and any balancing test where relying on legitimate interests.

Use of the website is voluntary. Name and email are required if you want a response; the career description is optional. If you choose not to give contact details, we cannot respond individually. Submitting an enquiry does not authorize unrelated promotional mailing. A recurring marketing newsletter would require a separate appropriate opt-in and an unsubscribe mechanism. We do not sell submitted contact details or use the information for automated hiring decisions.

4. Services, recipients and transfers

The current form posts to FormSubmit, which provides submission handling, spam filtering and forwarding to an operator-controlled email inbox. Its own controls, storage practices, subprocessors and possible data transfers are governed by its terms and privacy information. The form may use CAPTCHA services to prevent abuse.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Email handling uses the receiving mailbox provider. PDF/DOCX extraction in the optional experimental workspace may load browser-side libraries through a CDN. Depending on these providers' infrastructure, personal information may be processed outside the EEA; the specific locations and applicable safeguards must be verified before production launch.

5. How long information is kept

Contact enquiries are kept only while necessary to respond and administer access requests, followed by deletion or anonymization when no longer needed, subject to applicable legal obligations. A fixed operator-side deletion schedule is not yet implemented or verified; it must be defined before accepting production sign-ups at scale. The form provider may retain submission records for its own stated period; the exact currently applicable retention period and deletion guarantees require verification from its documentation or contract, separately from messages delivered into the receiving mailbox.

Retention action: the project must establish and implement a defined inbox deletion schedule for rejected, withdrawn and inactive enquiries before opening general sign-ups; it must also document any retention required to resolve disputes or comply with law. At present, no exact period is represented as operational.

Information stored in the experimental workspace's browser localStorage remains there until cleared by the browser or removed by the user or application. Uninstalling a website shortcut may not clear browser storage automatically.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may request access to information about you, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to relevant processing. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, including Spain's Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD), where applicable.

To exercise your rights before the dedicated mailbox is activated, use the contact form and begin the optional message with “Privacy request:”. Include the email address used in your original submission. We may need reasonable verification before changing or deleting records. This route currently uses the same third-party form processor.

7. Cookies, local storage and installation

The current public marketing page does not deliberately set analytics or advertising cookies. Hosting and external form providers may set their own technical or anti-abuse cookies when you interact with their services. Any non-essential cookies or third-party analytics added in future will require a separate assessment and appropriate consent controls before activation.

RoleHint's service worker stores static public assets in the browser Cache Storage to support installation and repeat visits. The browser's own storage controls can remove these files. The experimental workspace can save profile or CV-derived material locally; clearing the relevant browser site data removes that local copy, but it will not erase any separate message you explicitly sent through the early-access form.

8. Data incidents and safeguards

The project aims to restrict access to enquiries, reduce collection to necessary information and prevent sensitive CV uploads through the public intake form. Operational security procedures, documented processor arrangements and any applicable breach-notification processes must be reviewed and implemented for production. Where legally required, report qualifying breaches to the relevant supervisory authority and affected individuals in accordance with GDPR Articles 33 and 34.

9. AI, job decisions and profiling

The public website describes a proposed career-assistance service; its sample vacancies and evidence cards are illustrative. The preview does not make a legal or similarly significant automated decision about an individual. In future, any automated processing of uploaded CVs, ranking, inferred suitability, external job sourcing, or generated application drafts would need a revised notice describing the actual data flows, applicable lawful bases, retention, recipients, user controls and any relevant GDPR Article 22 safeguards before it is launched. This policy does not authorize undisclosed CV ingestion or AI training.

10. Security and limits

The public site is served over HTTPS. Applications pass through an external form processor, rather than a first-party secure account area. No online transmission is completely risk-free. The experimental workspace has no production authentication or account-level data recovery, and its demo opportunity data is fictional. Do not use it to submit sensitive documents or rely on it as an employment eligibility determination.

11. Children

RoleHint is designed for adults exploring employment. The early-access form is not intended for children, and we do not knowingly solicit children's personal information.

12. Changes to this notice

This policy may change as the prototype becomes a live service or its processors change. Material changes to collection, retention or marketing practices should be reflected on this page before those practices begin. The effective date above will be revised accordingly.
